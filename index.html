About Us

Creating moments that last a lifetime

Ever After Events is a Manchester-based planning studio for weddings and commercial events. We combine creative design with careful budgeting and supplier management, so every celebration feels personal and runs on time.

Whether it's a traditional ceremony, a multicultural wedding, a product launch or an awards night, our team plans, coordinates and manages the day from start to finish.

  • Full & partial planning packages
  • Trusted network of 60+ local suppliers
  • Transparent budgets, no hidden fees
  • On-the-day coordination team
